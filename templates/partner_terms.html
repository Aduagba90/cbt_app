📜 Partner terms — in plain language

No legal jargon. This is the whole agreement between you and PrepNova. Last updated: October 2026.

1. What this is

You are an independent sales partner of PrepNova, not an employee, agent on salary, or contractor. You promote PrepNova through your personal referral link, and we pay you commission on qualifying sales. You decide when and how much you work.

2. What it costs

Nothing, forever. Joining is free. We will never ask you for a registration fee, activation fee, or any payment to be a partner. If anybody asks you for money "to become a PrepNova partner", that person is a fraudster — report them to us.

3. How you earn

  • You earn 30% of a customer's first purchase made through your personal link (subscriptions, family plans and study-shop products).
  • Your link is remembered in the customer's browser for 30 days after they click it.
  • A "first purchase" means the customer has never successfully paid PrepNova before. Repeat purchases and renewals do not earn commission.
  • Commission shows in your dashboard immediately and becomes available for payout 14 days after the sale (to cover refunds).
  • Purchases you make yourself, or from a phone number or account that is clearly your own, do not earn commission.
  • Stocked-agent PIN purchases, school/lesson-centre bulk orders and sponsorships are negotiated separately and do not earn partner commission.

4. Getting paid

  • Request a payout once your available balance is ₦5,000 or more.
  • We process payouts weekly to the Nigerian bank account you gave us. It is your responsibility to keep the account details correct.
  • Commission is calculated on the amount the customer actually paid (before Paystack's processing fees).

5. What we expect from you

  • Be honest. Do not promise students guaranteed exam scores, "free access", or anything PrepNova does not offer.
  • Do not present yourself as a PrepNova staff member — you are a partner.
  • Do not spam. Repeated spam complaints can get your account suspended.
  • One partner account per person.

6. Fraud and abuse

Fake sales, self-purchases, chargeback schemes, stolen cards used "through your link", or any attempt to game the commission system will lead to a permanent ban and forfeiture of unpaid commission. We pay only from real product sales — that is what keeps this programme alive and honest.

7. Changes

We may update these terms or commission rates for future sales (never retroactively on money you have already earned). Material changes are announced on your dashboard.

8. Ending the partnership

You can stop any time — just stop sharing your link; anything already available in your balance above ₦5,000 will still be paid. We can end a partnership for rule violations (see 6) with unpaid clearing commissions cancelled.
