1. Who we are

PrepNova CBT ("PrepNova", "we", "us") provides online computer-based test practice for JAMB UTME, WAEC and university Post-UTME screening. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, phone number and, optionally, your school and state.
  • Activity data — exam attempts, answers, scores, bookmarks and practice history, so we can show you your progress and corrections.
  • Security data — IP address, browser type and login times, used to protect your account and enforce the single-device policy.
  • Payment data — your payments are processed by Paystack. We store only the transaction reference, plan and amount. We never see or store your card number.

3. How we use your data

To operate your account, deliver mock exams and results, process subscriptions, prevent fraud and cheating, send transactional emails (verification, password reset, receipts) and improve the service. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Cookies

We use a single secure session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

5. Data retention & your rights

You may update your details from your profile at any time. To delete your account and data, email {{ support_email }} from your registered address and we will action it within 14 days, except where records must be kept for accounting purposes.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, all traffic is encrypted in transit, and access to production data is restricted to authorised staff. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

7. Children

PrepNova is intended for students aged 13 and above. If you are under 18, please use the service with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.
