👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 How it works

  1. Pay once. No PrepNova account needed — you are buying for your children, not yourself.
  2. Receive {{ seats }} Access PINs instantly on screen and by email. Each PIN works once, on one child's account.
  3. Each child registers free at PrepNova, goes to Subscribe → "I have an Access PIN" and enters their PIN. Their full access starts immediately.
  4. Watch their progress free — each child can send you a Parent Link from their dashboard, and your PIN tracker page shows who has activated and who hasn't.

Each child gets the full platform

  • Unlimited practice questions with instant explanations
  • Their own results history, mistakes tracker and study plan
  • Both Offline Simulators free (JAMB + WAEC, ₦3,000 value)
  • Daily challenge, friend battles and leaderboards
