How it works

  1. You pay for the group here — no PrepNova account needed.
  2. We give you a join code (and email you a copy).
  3. The school shares it with the students — each one enters it under Subscribe → I have an Access PIN and gets full access.
  4. Anytime, open your private impact report: students joined, mocks written, average scores and improvement.

Proof your money worked

No donation to a school ever came with evidence. Your report shows real practice activity and score improvement — perfect for your association's next giving budget.
