How it works

  1. You pay for the plan here — no PrepNova account needed.
  2. We give you a gift PIN (and email you a copy).
  3. The student enters it under Subscribe → Have an Access PIN — new or existing account, both work.

Valid for 180 days

The PIN doesn't expire quickly — perfect for a birthday or Christmas gift before exam season.

Stacks with any plan

If the student already has access, the gifted days are added on top — nothing is wasted.
