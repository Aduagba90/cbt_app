How it works

  • One paper per week — the same questions for every student in your subjects.
  • Open 8 am – 8 pm on Saturday. Start any time in that window; you get the full 2 hours.
  • One attempt only. Your score joins the live ranking the moment you submit.
  • Ranking is by JAMB score /400, then by time. Ties are rare.
